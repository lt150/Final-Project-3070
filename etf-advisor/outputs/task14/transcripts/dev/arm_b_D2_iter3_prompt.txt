How to decide:

1. Capacity comes before willingness. How soon the money is needed, and how solid the person's financial position is, limit the profile no matter how much risk the person says they want.

   - Money needed in 3 to 10 years inclusive, or unstable income, or little to no emergency fund: do not go above balanced.

2. Within whatever capacity allows, use willingness to choose among the profiles still open: what the person would do after a large loss, whether they would rather protect what they have or grow it, and how much investing experience they have.

4. conservative, balanced and growth are the only profiles that exist, and growth is the most aggressive one available.
